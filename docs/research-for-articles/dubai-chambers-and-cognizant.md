### The market signal got sharper

The most important development is not another framework being added to a syllabus. It is this:

```text
2025–early 2026
AI TRAINING
    ↓
prompting
RAG
LLMs
copilots
frameworks

        becoming

late 2026
AGENTIC PROFESSIONAL PRACTICE
    ↓
delegation
autonomous execution
production agents
named job roles
operator roles
certification
governance
human accountability
organizational redesign
```

**Cognizant is especially important.** “Frontier Certified Engineer” plus “Frontier Business Operator” is evidence that the market is beginning to separate the person who **engineers autonomous capability** from the person who **operates and applies it in the business**. ([News | Cognizant Technology Solutions][2])

That maps beautifully to the direction of the SideFX curriculum:

```text
AGENTIC ENGINEER
designs the capability,
scenario, authority,
evidence and effect boundary

AGENTIC OPERATOR
works with the capability,
intervenes, supervises,
interprets evidence
and owns contextual judgment
```

And then your **wisdom layer** sits above both.

The SideFX opportunity is increasingly **not** to be the best course on building agents. There are going to be hundreds of those.

It is to teach the harder discipline:

> **How do humans engineer, delegate, govern, observe, challenge, and take responsibility for consequential effects produced by intelligent actors?**

That remains much less commoditized.

I did **not** find a credible new university degree launch or major new vendor credential dated after the prior check that was strong enough to add. The new movement over these two days is concentrated in **enterprise-scale workforce formation, role specialization, professional training, and governed-autonomy education**.
