01 / Cold Open

TARGET / She asked for an inspection.

She asked the agent to inspect a capability change. It queued publication instead. The command is ready. The agent has the tools. But the organization never authorized that change. What should happen before anything runs?

02 / Scenario

TARGET / Ready to publish. Authorized to inspect.

Meet the intended SideFX experience. One engineer. One proposed change. One action waiting at a boundary. The interlock connects the requested operation to the exact capability, session, and admitted authority. Execution stays pending while that decision is made.

03 / Human Experience

TARGET / Control should help her finish the work.

She needs the inspection today. Stopping everything leaves her stuck. Letting everything through surrenders control. A useful boundary tells the agent what it may do next, so the engineer can keep moving without silently approving more than she intended.

04 / Open the Event

CURRENT / What the capsule establishes today.

First, open the current capsule. Its stored adjudication expression checks the tool name. Dangerous-tool returns OPERATOR REQUIRED. The alternative branch returns ALLOW. Its activation and certification expressions return status fields. These are inspectable decision semantics. They do not yet demonstrate live interception of a tool action.

05 / Future Agentic Engineering Principle

TARGET / The agent cannot authorize itself.

Now project the stronger target. An attempted effect reaches the interlock before dispatch. Resolve the capability, authority, requested mechanic, and permitted boundary. PERMIT can reach execution. OPERATOR REQUIRED waits for a human. DENY or RESOLVE returns the reason and the next legal action.

06 / Contrast With Today

GAP / A decision becomes an enforced boundary.

The gap is the connection between deciding and enforcing. A returned label cannot stop a command by itself. The target operating environment must intercept the covered action, verify the exact session and authority, and dispatch only the permitted capability. The unfinished boundary marks the work that remains.

07 / SideFX Resolution

TARGET / Resolve the next legal action.

In our target simulation, publication stays pending. The interlock resolves the authorized next step: inspect the candidate. That inspection receives PERMIT and returns its report. Missing authority or coverage produces a hold with an exact reason. A consequential operation needing an operator waits for that separate decision.

08 / Evidence

GAP / What would close the gap?

Closure requires the same live session to deny an unmanaged probe and permit a declared read-only probe, with exact identity and coverage evidence. A receipt must connect the request, decision, and actual effect. The reference simulation teaches those relationships. It does not claim the current platform already enforces them.

09 / Takeaway

TARGET / The work continued. Authority stayed hers.

In the intended outcome, the engineer has the inspection she asked for. Publication remains pending until its own authority and proof close. Today we can inspect decision semantics. The future adds live interception before effects. That gap is the engineering runway: it tells us exactly what must become real.
