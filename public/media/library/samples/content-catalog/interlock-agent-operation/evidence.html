What would close the target experience

G01

Pre-effect interception and exact tool-path coverage

A requested unmanaged effect is denied before dispatch, with an observed no-effect receipt.

G02

Same-session two-sided certification

Deny and permit probes bind the same activation, session, workspace, adapter, daemon, policy, authority, and ordered time boundary.

G03

Authority and mechanic resolution beyond a tool-name branch

Unknown tools and authority mismatches never fall through to permission; exact admitted effect and next legal action are established.

G04

Authenticated operator control

An agent-supplied control request cannot grant approval, bypass a hook, or change its own policy.

G05

Decision-to-effect testimony

Each actual permitted effect binds its request, authority, decision, provider or mechanic, output, and lineage.

G06

Failure-path and freshness evidence

Unavailable adjudication, identity drift, malformed inputs, stale proofs, and uncovered paths hold before effects.
